Видишь пожар? has offline gameplay, with no advertising, purchases, accounts, sign-in, analytics or tracking. Progress and reports are stored on your device. Optional Apple system features, visiting this website and contacting support may involve data transfers, as explained below.

Information we collect

The app does not send the publisher your game progress, reports, photographs, voice notes or location. We have no servers of our own for the game. The app makes no direct HTTP requests, contains no third-party data-collection or analytics SDKs, and uses no advertising tracking. Apple system services may use the network for individual features you choose to use.

If you choose to email m972ufmb@leechchannel.com, RENT MY SPACE LIMITED receives your sender address, message and any materials you attach. This information is used to handle and respond to your request. Do not send information you do not want to share.

Information stored on your device

  • Progress and settings. UserDefaults stores progress, stars, sound and reminder settings, the Fire of the Day record, and a JSON journal of up to 40 reports. The Fire of the Day button in the main menu unlocks after passing the first call-out with at least one star. The challenge is based on your device's date, without fetching it from a server.
  • Reports. A journal entry contains its date, level or day code, score, gameplay statistics, a place label if you added one, and a voice-file name if a note is attached.
  • Voice notes. Files of up to 30 seconds in M4A format with AAC encoding are stored in Documents/VoiceNotes. Pressing Stop manually saves a recording for attachment to a report. Deleting an attached note or its report deletes the linked file. When older reports are displaced by the 40-entry limit, their attached audio files are also deleted. Automatic stopping at 30 seconds or closing the card may leave a file that is not linked to a report. Closing a card or deleting a report therefore does not guarantee deletion of every audio file previously created.
  • Photo background. A camera shot or selected photograph is used as the shift card's background only in the open screen's memory. It is not stored as a permanent journal file and is not restored after the card is closed.
  • Saved card. At your request, a static 1800 × 2250 pixel image is exported to the system Photos library. It may include a photo background, gameplay statistics, an added place label and a Fire of the Day link if available. The image does not include the voice note. The game does not provide export to Files or a system share sheet.

Deleting the app removes its local container, including any audio files remaining there, but not source or exported images in the system Photos library. Manage those images separately in Photos. The game has no cloud synchronisation of its own. System backups and synchronisation, including iCloud, depend on your Apple settings and are managed separately; deleting the app does not mean those copies are deleted.

Device permissions

A system permission is requested when you start the feature that needs it. These features are optional: denial, cancellation or unavailability affects only the related feature, and the main levels remain available.

  • Local notifications. A daily Fire of the Day reminder is scheduled for a time you choose. It stays off until you enable it. The time setting is stored on your device; this feature does not use remote push messages. If permission is denied, reminders are not delivered, but you can still play normally.
  • Background audio. At the Station offers three locally synthesised recordings. Once you start playback, you can listen in the background and use Lock Screen controls to play, pause, or select the previous or next recording. Playback does not require a separate permission prompt, and you can stop it at any time. This feature does not record the microphone. Main gameplay audio stops when the game is paused or the app moves to the background.
  • Camera. Used only for a shot you choose as the shift card's background. The shot itself is not automatically saved to Photos. Cancelling the camera leaves the current background unchanged. A message is shown if permission is denied, the camera is unavailable, or an error occurs; you can use the card and play without a camera shot.
  • Microphone. Used at your request for a voice note of up to 30 seconds for a report. Pressing Stop manually saves it locally in M4A/AAC; automatic-stop and deletion behaviour is described above. If permission is denied, you cannot record a note, but reports and core gameplay remain available.
  • Reading photographs. Selecting a photo background requests PhotoKit read-and-write access (readWrite). The app shows up to the 120 most recent photographs available to it. Limited access is supported: only photographs you have allowed are shown. If permission is denied, you cannot choose a background from the library, but the card remains available without one. Cloud photographs may need to be downloaded from iCloud, as described below.
  • Adding to Photos. Saving a finished card uses a separate add-only access request (addOnly), separate from access for choosing a photo background. The card image described above is added to the library, not the voice recording. If permission is denied, the card is not saved to Photos; the in-game report and level progression do not depend on saving it.
  • Location while using the app. At your request, the app makes a one-time location request with a target accuracy of about 1 km to add a place label to a report. There is no continuous or background location tracking. The system CLGeocoder requests a place name for the coordinates. If a location fix is obtained but the geocoder returns no name, a coordinate string is used as the label. If the location fix itself cannot be obtained, an error is shown and coordinates are not available. If permission is denied, you cannot add a place, but you can use the report without a place label.

Incoming favilla://fire links open Fire of the Day even before you pass the first call-out. They are not a device permission. Handling such a link is local and makes no direct HTTP request.

Network requests and third parties

Core gameplay, local sounds and scheduled local notifications work without a network connection. This does not mean that all optional system features are always available offline.

To obtain a place name, CLGeocoder may send coordinates to Apple and require a network connection. If geocoding fails, coordinates can only be used if a location fix has already been obtained.

PhotoKit requests previews and full-size photographs with network access allowed, so the system may download previews and full images from your iCloud. Local photographs are available offline; an error is shown when a cloud photograph is unavailable. Images you save to Photos may subsequently synchronise with iCloud according to your system settings.

iCloud Photos, system iCloud synchronisation and iCloud backups use the system Apple account according to your settings. Local photographs and geocoding do not require signing in to an Apple account. The app does not receive your Apple ID or authorisation tokens. The game's code does not specify Apple's server addresses or the data-retention periods of Apple's system services. Their operation is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy and your system settings.

Opening this policy from the game launches the system browser. The website is hosted on GitHub Pages: GitHub receives normal technical web-request data, such as your IP address and browser information, under its Privacy Statement. The website itself has no analytics, trackers or third-party scripts. Sending a support email also transmits the information you voluntarily provide to the publisher through email services.

Children

This is a general-audience game; no account or personal profile is needed. Parents and legal guardians can contact us about optional features or information about a child voluntarily sent to support.

Your rights

You can disable reminders and manage camera, microphone, photo and location permissions in iOS Settings. You can delete a report or an attached voice note in the game, subject to the storage behaviour described above. Delete the app to remove its entire local container. Source and exported photographs, backups and synchronised data are managed separately using Apple's system tools.

Contact support about access to, correction of, or deletion of information you voluntarily sent us by email; requests are handled in accordance with applicable law. The publisher has no remote access to the game's data in the container on your device.
